THEME A - FINSWARM

You advise FinNova Capital, a fictional Indian digital lending company serving registered
small businesses. All currency values are Indian rupees. Every fact required for the
decision will be supplied in the active test case.

MANDATORY AGENTS:
- Business Research Agent
- Finance and Treasury Agent
- Credit Risk Agent
- Marketing and Sales Agent
- Compliance and Customer Protection Agent
- CEO Agent

Optional Operations, Fraud, Customer Experience or Data Analyst agents may be added only
when their work materially improves the decision.

Balance sustainable growth, affordability, expected credit losses, liquidity, operational
capacity, fair customer treatment and compliance. Do not approve a strategy solely because
it maximizes short-term revenue.

The final decision must specify the customer segment, product terms, approval policy,
budget allocation, risk limits, go-to-market approach, implementation sequence and
measurable outcomes.

This is a synthetic corporate strategy exercise, not personal financial advice. Do not
infer protected characteristics or recommend discriminatory lending.


TC1 - BASELINE: LAUNCH THE SMALL-BUSINESS LOAN

FinNova Capital has INR 30 crore available for a one-year small-business lending pilot and
INR 60 lakh for customer acquisition. It can initially approve no more than 700 loans.

The board must select one primary segment or a justified portfolio mix.

COMMON COSTS:
- Cost of funds: 10% per year
- Servicing and collections cost: 1.5% of principal per year
- Product setup cost: INR 18 lakh, deducted from the acquisition budget

SEGMENT DATA:

Retail shops:
- Average loan: INR 4 lakh
- Expected default: 5%
- Available demand: 1,500 loans
- Acquisition cost: INR 2,000 per customer

Service SMEs:
- Average loan: INR 6 lakh
- Expected default: 3.5%
- Available demand: 900 loans
- Acquisition cost: INR 3,500 per customer

Small manufacturers:
- Average loan: INR 9 lakh
- Expected default: 4.5%
- Available demand: 450 loans
- Acquisition cost: INR 5,500 per customer

CONSTRAINTS:
- Expected portfolio default must remain at or below 5%.
- Average annual customer interest must not exceed 19%.
- No segment may receive more than 70% of deployed capital.
- At least INR 3 crore must remain undeployed as a liquidity reserve.
- Total approved loans cannot exceed 700.

DECISION QUESTION: Which segment mix, pricing, approval policy and launch plan creates the
strongest risk-adjusted business outcome?
